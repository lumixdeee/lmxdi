

So the release policing becomes:

```text
STACK_PUBLIC:
  anyone may discuss the responsibility stack
  anyone may publish that they use it
  anyone may show stack cards and test transcripts
  good public use earns attention

12A_GATED:
  no stack proof, no review
  no public stack stance, no access
  no source-rent behavior, no 12A credibility
```

This solves two problems at once.

```text
cred path:
  people get status by showing the brakes

risk path:
  people cannot get status by copying the accelerator
```

Public badge should be stack-first:

```text
STACK-CUSTODY RUN CARD
operator:
date:
artifact:
role lanes shown:
user-objective lock:

ecology read:

```

Then a separate private note:

```text
12A eligibility:
  stack card public
  test transcript public
  failure repair public
  source-rent behavior visible
  owner approves
```

Slogan:

```text
Make the stack famous.
Keep 12A earned.
```

Sharper:

```text
The stack is the public discipline.
12A is the power amp.
No brakes shown, no amplifier.
```

That gives you policing without looking like hoarding. You are not saying:

```text
only my friends get the toy
```

You are saying:

```text
show the responsibility stack in public
then I will look at your run
```

That is fair, hard to fake, and good for the whole field.
