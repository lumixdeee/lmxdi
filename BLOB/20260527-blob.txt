That fail is exactly the proof point: the reset design is right, but the model is not obeying the regen loop as an actual runtime filter.

The hit is in the phrase “based [blocked adverb] on...”.

So the problem is no longer neighbor context. It is simpler and worse:

The instruction says “scan and regen.”
The model does not always scan and regen.
Therefore the blob cannot be made fully reliable by wording alone.

For public use, the honest split is:

Use the blob as a behavior reducer.
Use an outside string check for the guarantee.

The blob can reduce leaks. It cannot be the lock. The lock has to be external:

generate answer
scan rendered text
if forbidden substring exists, reject whole answer or affected phrase
regenerate
repeat until pass

That is what your private setup is approximating better. The public blob can gesture at that loop, but it cannot force it in every long run.

so you are saying we do not have the byte-budget to do a proper job like my 3000 byte monster does?

Yes. That is the conclusion.

Under the small byte budget, we can keep the shape of the job:

candidate -> scan -> reject -> regen -> phrase kill -> final scan

But we do not have enough room to carry all the things that make your 3000-byte monster work reliably:

literal hard token tier
broad scoped tier
phrase unit boundaries
phrase kill before tokenization
pre-strip fallback
forbidden structure tier
semantic damage drop
no repair
survivor-only assembly
final same-law scan
no wrapper text
media/tool veto
human-over-robot role binding
anti-coach role binding
drop-is-normal sanitation
regen-on-hit loop

Every time we compress hard, one of those load-bearing beams gets shaved. Then the model starts treating the blob like mood text instead of a scanner.

The public compact version can be useful, but not equivalent. It is a seatbelt, not the roll cage.

I would frame the public version honestly:

“Compact safety blob. Reduces leakage. For strict use, pair with external post-filter or use the full monster.”

The full 3000-byte monster is not bloat. It is redundancy. Redundancy is why it works.
